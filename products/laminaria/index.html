Laminaria digitata

Dried and milled kelp in three published grades. Discuss the application and destination market with Thorverk before specifying this material.

GradesSpecificationDocumentsQuestions

Three grades.
One starting point.

The published catalogue lists three grade labels. Confirm the current sieve specification and intended application with Thorverk.

Choose the application with care.

Thorverk states that its Laminaria should not go into animal feed and is not recommended for human consumption in the EU. Requirements differ by market. Discuss your intended use and destination with the team before purchase.

How do I choose a grade?+

Start by discussing your intended application and market. Thorverk can then confirm grade availability and current sieve specifications.

Can I request a sample?+

Use the sample enquiry option and include your intended application. Sample availability, quantities and shipping arrangements are confirmed by the team.

Are the published analyses guaranteed batch values?+

No. Typical analyses are reference information. Ask about the current specification and the testing or batch documentation you need.
