Our process

Local seaweed. Local geothermal heat.
A process shaped by the place we work.

HarvestDryingMillingTraceability
01

Harvest

Ascophyllum grows along the intertidal shores of Breiðafjörður. Laminaria is gathered from deeper waters.

For Ascophyllum, Thorverk describes cutting above the growth point and allowing at least four years before returning to the same ground. Harvesting areas are tracked and the work follows resource permissions and research guidance.

Read Thorverk’s published harvesting account ↗︎
02

Drying

After landing, the seaweed is chopped and dried at Reykhólar.

Local geothermal water passes through heat exchangers to warm the drying air. This is the distinctive link between Thorverk’s raw material and its Icelandic setting.

03

Milling

Milling and sieving produce different textures, from fine powder to coarser flakes.

The catalogue lists five Ascophyllum grades and three Laminaria grades. Choose a starting point, then confirm the specification for your process.

Explore the grades↗︎
04

Traceability

Thorverk’s Laminaria declaration describes harvesting-location tracking and batch numbers tied to the drying process. Ask for the documents needed to evaluate your particular shipment.

What to include in an enquiry

  • Species and preferred grade
  • Application and destination country
  • Approximate quantity and timing
  • Current certificate and testing requirements
